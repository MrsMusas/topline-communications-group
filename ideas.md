# TLCG Website Foundation — Design Direction

## Three possible approaches

### 1. Verdant Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet-luxury editorial system where rich forest green, warm paper, and narrow gold rules feel as considered as a private members’ journal. The experience is immersive, confident, and deliberately paced.

**Probability:** 0.06

### 2. Brass & Stone
**Very Brief Intro:** A material-led corporate identity inspired by architectural wayfinding, brushed brass, and monolithic spatial composition. It gives TLCG an enduring and infrastructural presence.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. The Event Field
**Very Brief Intro:** A gallery-forward composition that balances documentary energy with a restrained gallery atmosphere. It suggests the motion, scale, and human texture of live experience without feeling theatrical.

**Probability:** 0.08

---

## Chosen approach: Verdant Ledger

### Design Movement
**New Luxury Editorialism**: a contemporary interpretation of heritage publishing, pairing near-black forest green with uncoated-paper warmth, disciplined typography, and art-directed intervals of scale.

### Core Principles
The site will feel composed rather than decorated. Its visual hierarchy will rely on deliberately oversized type, expansive negative space, and clear moments of pause. Deep forest sections will create cinematic contrast, while ivory editorial sections keep dense information legible and human. Gold will never be a broad fill; it will act as a precise instrument for rules, highlights, and navigation cues. Images will be framed like campaign stills, with enough breathing room to accommodate TLCG’s future photography.

### Color Philosophy
Forest green represents strategic depth and focus. Warm ivory creates the tactile calm of premium paper stock rather than the sterility of white. Muted gold is a sparing signifier of craft, value, and momentum; its restraint makes it memorable. The primary ownable color is **TLCG Verdant**, a blackened forest green (#10261E).

### Layout Paradigm
The design will use a **vertical editorial procession** rather than a traditional centered-grid page. Sections will alternate between full-bleed dark fields, left-aligned narrative gutters, offset image panels, and slim side annotations. The frame feels like turning pages in a substantial book: clear chapter changes, deliberate reveal points, and no repetitive card walls.

### Signature Elements
The visual language will repeatedly use fine gold horizon rules, oversized outline chapter numerals, and an interrupted “reading line” that animates across titles as the visitor moves down the page. Deep image windows will carry a translucent forest overlay so placeholder imagery looks intentional rather than generic.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interaction should reward attention without demanding it. Navigation will remain explicit and usable, while hover states reveal a measured directional response: rules extend, labels tighten, and image crops settle forward. Each interaction should read as an editorial cue, not a software control.

### Animation
Motion will be limited to opacity and transform. Section labels and primary headlines will rise gently into view with 40–70ms staggering; broad gold rules will draw once per section. Images will use a restrained scale settle on reveal and a 220ms hover drift. The page will use native smooth scrolling and a reduced-motion alternative that removes nonessential reveals. No looping decoration, parallax spectacle, or bouncing behavior will be used.

### Typography System
**Cormorant Garamond** will create high-contrast, expressive display headlines and major numerals. **Manrope** will support it with a sharply legible modern sans for navigation, labels, body copy, and form inputs. Headlines will use tight tracking and controlled line length; all-caps supporting labels will use broad letter-spacing and smaller scale.

### Brand Essence
**Top Line Communications Group is a strategic, human-centered communications partner that turns important moments into experiences with real presence.** The personality is **assured, discerning, and alive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be distilled, declarative, and spacious. Calls to action should be invitational but decisive. Microcopy should use short, clear utility language rather than marketing filler.

> “Make the moment matter.”

> “Start a considered conversation.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The official TLCG logo is a protected brand asset and will be presented exactly as supplied. The interface will treat it as a gold emblem set against calm, high-contrast fields; it must never be recreated, redrawn, restyled, or replaced.

### Signature Brand Color
**TLCG Verdant — #10261E**

## Style Decisions

- No placeholder, provisional, or build-status language will appear in the live visual design. Copy remains concise, brand-specific, and grounded in strategic communications, human presence, live moments, messaging, audiences, and execution.
- Future imagery will favour refined human presence around strategy, production, documentation, and live experience rather than generic luxury architecture alone.
- TLCG’s recurring editorial motif is a fine gold reading line paired with a chapter number. It appears across major section labels as a restrained system cue.
